Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how P Company uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website, remember your preferences, and improve your experience.

🍪 What Are Cookies?

  • Small text files placed on your device by your browser when you visit a website.
  • They help websites function, provide security, remember preferences, and measure performance.

⚙️ Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: required for core features like sign in and cart.
  • Preference cookies: remember choices such as language and filters.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand traffic and improve the site.
  • Marketing cookies: only used if you opt-in to personalized offers.

🛠 How We Use Cookies

  • Keep items in your cart and maintain your session.
  • Save your preferences for a smoother experience.
  • Measure page performance and fix issues.

🔒 Managing Cookies

  • You can accept, reject, or delete cookies via your browser settings.
  • Disabling some cookies may impact site features such as checkout or login.
